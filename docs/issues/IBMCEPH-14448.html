Root Cause Analysis
MDS stuck in Replay state with 90GB uncommitted journal after upgrade to 8.1z5 due to client cap revoke delays during MDS scale-down causing internal heartbeat timeout.
ROOT CAUSE: During ceph orch upgrade to 8.1z5, cephadm scales down the multi-MDS filesystem to 1 active MDS before upgrading MDS daemons. This triggers subtree export from rank 1 to rank 0. A Ganesha NFS client (client.72745998) was slow to respond to cap revoke requests, preventing subtree freeze from completing. The export operation retried repeatedly without converging, causing the MDSRank thread to time out its internal heartbeat (15s threshold), which led to skipped beacons, MON marking MDS as laggy, and replacement with standby. The replacement MDS then had to replay 90GB of journal entries. Greg Farnum identified batch getattr operations potentially bypassing freeze operations as a contributing factor. The issue occurred despite all PGs being active+clean at the time of scale-down.

FIX STATUS: Still assigned/open. Under active investigation by Venky Shankar and Greg Farnum. Suspected client-side bug in cap revoke handling or batch operation interaction with freeze.
