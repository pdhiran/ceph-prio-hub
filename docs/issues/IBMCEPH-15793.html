Root Cause Analysis
### Root Cause Analysis:
SUMMARY: RGW lifecycle (LC) processing stuck with 234 buckets in PROCESSING state, unable to complete
ROOT CAUSE: The RGW lifecycle processing is unable to complete its workload within the configured processing window. With 234 LC operations stuck in PROCESSING state, the issue appears to be a combination of: (1) insufficient parallelism for the scale of delete operations (millions of objects daily, 1-day retention), (2) possible LC shard contention with current shard count, and (3) a known RGW segfault in RGWIndexCompletionManager::create_completion() during object delete in LC cleanup (IBMCEPH-12966/IBMCEPH-14805, targeted for 8.1z7). Engineering recommended: expand LC window to 24x7, reshard LC shards to reset partition structure, and potentially increase shard count.
KEY EVIDENCE:
- 234 LC operations stuck in PROCESSING (184 from yesterday, 38 from today, 12 from 2 days ago)
- 7 threads per RGW instance, 8 RGW instances across 4 servers
- Users delete millions of objects daily, 1-day retention policy
- radosgw-admin lc process --bucket=<name> shows brief peak then stalls
- Known segfault IBMCEPH-12966 in LC cleanup path on 8.1z6
- Version: 8.1z6 (19.2.1-366)
FIX STATUS: In progress. Recommendations: (1) Change LC schedule to 24x7 (00:01-23:59), (2) Run 'radosgw-admin lc reshard fix' to clear stuck processing state, (3) Increase LC shard count, (4) Upgrade to 8.1z7 for segfault fix. Customer action pending -- site requires physical access.
